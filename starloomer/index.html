The connected world

Under the hood: one shared CORE.

The original four Starloomer prototypes are Python/FastAPI apps designed around a shared SQLite state, not disconnected arcade pages. Browser Space Ace and Signal Sweep use their original game interfaces with local pilot saves, while the lightweight Junk Run preview is a separate interpretation. None of these browser versions modifies the live shared crew records.

CREW & PROGRESSION

Shared crew identity, ranks, XP, stats, and gear. A mission should matter to the next one.

STATION & RESOURCES

Salvaged parts, Signal Data, station integrity, unlocks, and missions that influence other games.

BROADCAST INTEGRATION

Carrier Channel 116, OBS, Discord, on-air incidents and audience participation are part of the longer vision.

Beyond the working prototypes

Eleven planned broadcast games.

The original Carrier Channel 116 design document sketches a larger universe: Keep 116 on the Air, Deep Scan, Mining Ops, Signal Intrusion, Cargo Run, Crew Duel, Robot Care, Shore Leave, Airlock, Signal Hunt, and Junk Run. These are design concepts, not all finished applications; Signal Sweep, Engineering Panic, and Space Ace are implemented additions to the actual CORE.
