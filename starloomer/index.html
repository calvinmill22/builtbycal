01 / The universe

More than a game clan.

ORIGIN / ELITE DANGEROUS

The fleet carrier that became a home.

At the heart of Starloomer is CMDR Calvinus1337 and the namesake fleet carrier The Starloomer [WNJ-T4Q]: a mobile staging point for exploration, hauling, colonization logistics, mentoring, expeditions, and whatever questionable plan Command has authorized this week.

The community evolved into Starloomer Carrier Command [L00M], an independent squadron built around practical teamwork, shared adventures, and making the galaxy feel a bit less empty.

Independent squadronCarrier operationsMulti-crew adventuresLow-ego collaboration
WORLD-BUILDING / BROADCAST / SOFTWARE

Then we started building the universe around it.

Starloomer became a creative umbrella: community streams, tools, fictional broadcast personalities, custom music, shared lore, and an interconnected suite of sci-fi minigames.

The real-world game community and the fictional station fiction overlap by design. Carrier Channel 116 is the deliberately chaotic entertainment arm; Starloomer Carrier Command remains the player community and operations hub.

“Order from Chaos.”

The game setting is rooted in Elite Dangerous. Starloomer's characters, station fiction, interactive games and branding are independently created community projects.

04 / Carrier Channel 116

Space television.
Questionable management.

What if the audience wasn't just watching a stream—what if they worked on the station?

Carrier Channel 116 is Starloomer's fictional pirate broadcast operation, with a Discord watch-along culture, sci-fi nonsense, crew departments, planned on-air incidents and games that make viewers part of the story.

The fictional cast has included Basil (the sassy onboard AI), Larry (air traffic control) and Emilie (comms). The station's department roster spans Engineering, Science, Flight Ops, Security, Broadcast, and Cargo & Salvage.

06 / People and allies

Not a solo expedition.

Streamers, pilots and friends.

The Starloomer idea is to put multiple perspectives in one galaxy: longtime pilots and rookies, explorers and freight captains, creators and spectators. One convoy can become several streams, a Discord watch party, a mission story, and tomorrow's ridiculous in-universe news report.

Our earlier fleet notes also included friendly captains and associated vessels such as Gander / T.C. Washington, Shua / Titan Mk I, and Cami / Mom & Pop Gaming / Camino / Naglfar. Those are historical community connections, not a claim of current diplomatic or carrier status.
