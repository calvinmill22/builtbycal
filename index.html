A different engineering path

Operations experience. Engineering curiosity.

I came to technology through years of hands-on technical production and equipment operations. That background trained me to solve problems under pressure and think about the whole system—not just one piece.

Today I design and operate independent applications, local AI infrastructure, and self-hosted services. I use AI-assisted development where useful while owning the requirements, testing, configuration, integration, and troubleshooting of what I build.

I'm interested in business systems, infrastructure operations, internal tooling, and roles that connect technical execution with people who need dependable solutions.

Good engineering isn't about the fanciest system. It's about building the right system, and making sure it actually works.
